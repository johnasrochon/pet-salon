Our Story

Paws & Claws was founded in 2018 with one goal in mind: to create a loving, safe, and relaxing environment where pets can look and feel their best. From stylish grooming to calming baths and gentle care, our team treats every furry friend like family.

Our Mission

We believe that grooming should be more than just a service—it’s an experience. Our mission is to promote pet wellness, comfort, and happiness through gentle care, organic products, and lots of love.
